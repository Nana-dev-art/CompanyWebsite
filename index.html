About us

Technology that fits the way you work.

What OGNY Tech is

OGNY Tech is a software development company that designs and builds practical applications for businesses and organisations, from internal tools to customer-facing systems.

Why I started it

Many organisations still depend on manual processes, scattered spreadsheets and software that was never built for them. I founded OGNY Tech to build software that fits the work people actually do.

I’m the founder and lead developer, so you work directly with the person building your software.

Vision

To be a trusted software partner for organisations that want technology that genuinely works for them.

Mission

To deliver reliable, practical and well-supported software that saves time, reduces errors and helps organisations run better.

Problems I want to solve

  • Slow, error-prone manual processes
  • Disconnected tools and duplicated work
  • Off-the-shelf software that doesn’t fit the business

My approach

Understand the problem first, build in small steps, show working software often, and keep supporting it after launch.

How we work
